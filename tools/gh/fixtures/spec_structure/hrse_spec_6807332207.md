### TC1 — Window start and exit 2 (AC1, AC2)
No `catchup_marked` event and no `--since`: exit code 2, message "The cleanup has not run…", NO table. With `--since`: a table of five rows in order, each with value, the two counts, target and status. With a seeded `catchup_marked` event and no `--since`: the window starts at the start of that event's day in the operator's zone and spans 42 days. Class: ac.

### TC2 — Measure 1, coming back (AC3)
Person decisions in three ISO weeks; an `undone`, `expired`, `auto_committed` and `section_expanded` event in a fourth week; a `confirmed` by `system:cleanup` in a fifth. Reads 3, NOT 4 or 5. A decision at 23:30 Sunday operator time (Monday UTC) lands in that Sunday's ISO week. Class: ac.

### TC3 — Measure 2, asks decided not expired (AC4, ruling B)
Two expired, six decided by a person, one decided by `script:x`, one expired-then-reopened-then-decided row (counts once, as decided), three decided rows with a `catchup_batch_id`. Reads 2 of 9; excludes the script row and the three cohort rows. Class: ac.

### TC4 — Measure 3, catch-up (AC5, AC9)
Ten cohort rows, one deadline: four decided by a person before it, one after, one decided then undone, four open. Reads 4 of 10. Re-run with `--as-of` after the deadline: status `missed`, value unchanged. Class: ac.

### TC5 — Measure 4, how little reaches the operator (AC6)
Rows enqueued in the window: two `ask`, one `ask` with an `expired` event of reason `not_asked`, three `auto_commit`, two `expire`, one `profile_prompt`, one `off_stream`, one with no `disposition`. With no dropped counts: 2 of 10, the no-disposition row not counted. Then add Activities (count 6 first recorded in the window; count 4 before the window; neither property): reads 2 of 16, NOT 2 of 10 or 2 of 20; the note shows 10 rows and 6 dropped at source. A dropped fact is never in the numerator; a `profile_prompt` row is in the denominator only. Class: ac.

### TC6 — Measure 5, auto-commit errors (AC7, ruling C)
Four auto-commits on meeting A, two on B. `section_expanded` for `added_automatically` on A after its commits; none on B; `closed_without_asking` on B. Undo one on A and one on B. Reads 1 of 4, note says 2 not seen. An auto-commit on A after the expansion does NOT count as seen. An `undone` with a `batch_id` does NOT raise the measure. An auto-commit whose `auto_committed` actor is `system:cleanup`, then undone, leaves the reading at 1 of 4. Class: ac.

### TC7 — Zero denominators and statuses (AC8, AC9)
Only a `catchup_marked` event seeded: measures 2-5 print `not measurable`, NOT `0%` and NOT `met`. Only a dropped count in the window and no row: measure 4 reads 0 of that count. Before the window ends every status is `open` (measure 1 may be `met` at 5 weeks). At `--as-of` 43 days after start each status is `met` or `missed`. Class: ac.

### TC8 — By-kind lines (AC10)
The two by-kind blocks group by `candidate_type` and split `transcript_extraction` rows by the first option's item kind; an unreadable `options_json` yields `transcript_extraction/unknown`. Measure 4's block ends with `dropped at source`. Class: ac.

### TC9 — Read-only (AC11)
Node and relationship counts equal before and after a run. A write attempted through a READ-mode session is refused by the driver. A regex over the seven statement constants' values finds none of `CREATE|MERGE|DELETE|SET|REMOVE|DETACH`. Class: ac.
